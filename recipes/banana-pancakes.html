Banana Pancakes

Photo by Kelsey Curtis on Unsplash

Fluffy banana pancakes made from scratch with mashed ripe bananas that are ready in minutes for a delicious twist on ordinary pancakes!

Wake up on the right side of the bed with a stack of sweet, cozy, and simple banana pancakes. This top-rated banana pancake recipe is easy to make and it comes together in just 15 minutes, so you don't have to wake up early to enjoy a satisfying breakfast. Learn how to make, store, and serve the best banana pancakes ever.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed

Steps:

  1. Gather all the ingredients
  2. Combine the dry ingredients of flour, white sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl
  3. In a separate bowl combine the wet ingredients of egg, milk, vegetable oil, and bananas
  4. pour the contents of the dry ingredient flour mixture bowl into the wet ingredients banana mixture bowl and fold with a spatula to combine. The batter will be slightly lumpy.
  5. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium heat. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle using about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake
  6. Cook pancakes until they are golden brown on each side, about 3 to 5 minutes per side.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!
